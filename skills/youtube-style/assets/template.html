    About the channel

    Halfmoon Kitchen is a home cooking channel filmed in a small flat kitchen in Penang. Noor started recording weeknight dinners in 2019 for friends who had just moved out, and it slowly became a channel about cooking slowly enough to understand what you are doing, even when you only have half an hour.

    A new recipe goes up every Thursday, a short technique most days, and a cook-along every other Sunday. Every recipe is tested at least three times, by at least two of us, with an ordinary oven and ordinary pans.

    The channel is paid for by members, courses and a few sponsorships a year. Sponsored videos are always marked, and we only work with things we already use.
