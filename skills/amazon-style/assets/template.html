                    Delivery

                    When will my order arrive?

                    Each product page shows a delivery date for your postal code before you buy. Most orders placed before midnight arrive the next day.

                    How much does delivery cost?

                    Delivery is free on orders of $35 or more. Below that it costs $5.99 per order.

                    Returns and refunds

                    How do I return something?

                    Open Your orders, choose "Return or replace items" and print the label, or show the code at a drop-off point. Returns are free within 30 days.

                    When do I get my refund?

                    We refund to the original payment method within two business days after the item reaches us.

                    Payment

                    Which payment methods do you accept?

                    Credit and debit cards and Pennyrow gift cards. You are charged when your order ships.

                    About Pennyrow

                    A general store that fits in a search box

                    We sell the everyday things a household runs on, at prices we would pay ourselves, and we bring them to your door the next day.

                    Fair prices, shown plainly

                    The price you see includes everything but delivery, and delivery is free from $35.

                    Tomorrow, not someday

                    Every product page tells you the day it arrives before you put it in your cart.

                    Returns without questions

                    Thirty days, free, with the label already in the box.

                    How we choose what we sell

                    We started with a short list of things people buy every week and asked which ones were hard to find at a fair price. We still work that way: a product joins the shelf when it is useful, well made and priced so you do not need to wait for a sale.

                    Our own line, Pennyrow Basics, covers the plain essentials. Everything else comes from makers we can name on the product page.
