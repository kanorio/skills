      1. I

        Lift

        Hold it by the knot at the top and set it in your spoon.

      2. II

        Pierce

      Our story

      Lo Wen opened a counter on Ferry Lane in the spring of 1979, with six stools, one steamer stack and a window onto the street so people could watch him fold.

      The window is still there. So is the rule he made that first year: every dumpling is folded the same way on a quiet Tuesday as on New Year's Eve. Today forty cooks fold in four houses, and each of them learned at that window.

      Visit Ferry Lane

      FiveSteps

      The same, every time

      1. I

        Knead

        Flour and warm water, rested for an hour under cloth.

      2. II

        Roll

        Six centimetres across, thinner at the edge than the middle.

      3. III

        Fill

        Twenty-one grams, weighed, with a cube of jellied broth.

      4. IV

        Fold

        Eighteen pleats, closed with a small twist at the top.

      5. V

        Steam

        Five minutes over a fierce boil, straight to the table.

      A dumpling should be folded the same way on a quiet Tuesday as on New Year's Eve.
      Lo Wen, founder

      Seasonal

      The crab dumplings are back for winter

      The first crabs of the season arrived on Monday. Until the end of February, every house folds them by hand, a few baskets at a time.

      Every autumn we wait for the same message from the boats in Harrow Bay: the water has turned cold and the crabs are full. This year it came on a Sunday night, and by Monday morning the kitchen at Ferry Lane smelled of ginger and crab.

      Picking the roe is slow work. Two cooks spend the morning at it, and the roe is folded into the pork filling the same day, never frozen. That is why we only make a few baskets at a time, and why they sometimes run out before dinner.

      How to order them

      Crab and pork soup dumplings are on the menu at all four houses until the end of February. Ask for them when you sit down; we cannot hold them with a reservation.

      • Ten to a basket, $19
      • Best with a pot of mountain oolong
      • Not suitable for anyone with a shellfish allergy
      The roe goes into the filling the same day it is picked. Nothing else tastes like the first week.

      We will write again when the season ends. Until then, come early.

      Tables

      Do you take walk-ins?

      Yes. Half of every room is kept for walk-ins. Leave your name at the door and we will call you when a table is ready.

      How long do you hold a booking?

      Fifteen minutes. If you are running late, call the house and we will do our best.

      Can we bring a large group?

      Up to eight can book online. For nine to twelve, the private room at Lantern Row is yours; write to us.

      Food

      What can vegetarians eat?

      About a third of the menu. Use the vegetarian filter on the menu, and tell your server so the steamer is wiped first.

      Do you cater for allergies?

      Ask your server before you order. Our kitchens use wheat, soy, sesame, egg and shellfish every day, so we cannot promise any dish is free of them.

      Can I watch the folding?

      Every house has a window kitchen facing the room or the street. The best seats are at Ferry Lane.

      Gifts

      How long do the frozen dumplings keep?

      Three months in the freezer. Steam them straight from frozen for eight minutes.

      Do you deliver?

      Not yet. Every gift is collected from the house you choose, two days after you order.
