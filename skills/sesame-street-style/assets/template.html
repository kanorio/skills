Safe, small and ad-free

The same rules apply in the studio and on this website.

  • Up to eight children in a class, with two teachers.
  • Grown-ups stay for the whole first visit.
  • The kids' pages have no ads, no outside links and nothing to buy.
  • We only ask grown-ups for contact details, never children.

Questions grown-ups ask

What should my child wear?

Clothes that can get messy and shoes that come off easily. We have aprons, spare socks and wipes.

Can I stay during the class?

Grown-ups stay for Little Makers and for every first visit. For older groups there is a reading corner by the door.

What if we miss a week?

Tell us by the day before and we will offer a place in another class that week.

Is the studio step-free?

Yes. Everything is on the ground floor, with space to park a pram inside.

About us

A studio full of shapes, stories and glue

Puddlejump started as one Saturday class in a borrowed hall. Two teachers, a box of paper shapes and a puppet show about a circle called Pim.

What we care about

Play first

Children learn the most when they are busy having fun, so every class starts with a game.

Small groups

Eight children and two teachers, so nobody waits long for a turn or a hug.

Calm screens

Our games are short and end on their own. No streaks, no ads, no pop-ups.

Privacy notice

Last updated 1 September. Sample text: replace it with your own notice.

What we collect

When a grown-up books a class or sends a message, we keep their name, email, phone number if given, and their child's first name and age. Children never fill in forms on this website.

The games and stories

The games, stories and colour-ins run in your browser. They do not ask for names, do not save drawings and do not show ads.

How long we keep it

Booking details are kept for the term and deleted twelve months after your child's last class.

Your choices

Write to hello@puddlejump.example to see, correct or delete what we hold about you or your child.
