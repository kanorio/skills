    Earn the dark

    Every weekday at 5:30, eleven runners meet under the same street lamp. In January it is dark for the first forty minutes. Nobody has missed a week since October.

    The route never changes: river path out, bridge, river path back, nine kilometers. The pace is set so the slowest person can still talk. "If you can't hold a conversation, you're racing," says Ines, who started the group with a message to four neighbors.

    What they wear

    Layers that shed heat by the second kilometer, gloves you can take off with your teeth, and anything that throws light back at a car. The Tempo 3 shows up on most feet, mainly because it doesn't ask for a warm-up mile.

    Nobody claps at 5:30. That's the point.
    Ines Okafor, night crew

    Why it works

    No app, no leaderboard, one rule: if you say you are coming, come. When someone is injured, they still show up and hold the thermos.

    Our story

    Gear for people who show up

    Kordo started in 2019 with one running shoe and a promise to keep making it better instead of making it new. We still work that way: fewer products, longer lives, and a club run in every city we sell in.

    What we stand for

    1. 01

      Made to move

      Every product is tested by our club runners for three months before it goes on sale. If they stop wearing it, we stop making it.

    2. 02

      Made to last

      Send any Kordo shoe back for one free resole. Clothing gets free repairs for two years.

    3. 03

      Made for everyone

      Sizes from XS to 3XL in every piece of clothing, and shoes in regular and wide fits at the same price.

    Free club runs every Tuesday and Saturday. Every pace welcome.
