The twenty-minute reset our cleaners swear by

Five rooms, one timer, and the order that stops you cleaning the same surface twice.

Ask any of our cleaners how they get a flat back in shape between deep cleans and you will hear the same thing: work top to bottom, dry to wet, and never go back into a room you have finished.

Start with the bin bag

Walk the whole home once with a bag and a basket. Rubbish in the bag, things in the wrong room in the basket. Do not stop to put anything away yet.

Then the order that matters

  • Dust high surfaces before low ones
  • Kitchen counters before the floor
  • Bathroom last, so you leave with the wet cloths
  • Floors from the far corner towards the door

Set a timer for twenty minutes. When it rings, stop. The point is a reset you will actually repeat, not a perfect home once a month.

A regular clean takes care of it every week or two.
