      A year of slow nights

      What forty listening nights in four cities taught us about rooms, volume and leaving early.

      IHInes Halloran, sound lead

      The first night was in a borrowed rehearsal room with eleven chairs and one speaker that hummed. Nine people came. Two fell asleep, which we took as a good sign, and one stayed afterwards to ask whether we could do it again somewhere warmer.

      A year later we have hosted forty nights in Lisbon, Seoul, Taipei and Berlin. The format barely changed: lights low, phones in a basket by the door, one long set from a single room, and nobody on stage.

      The room matters more than the speaker

      We spent the first months chasing better equipment. It helped less than we hoped. What changed every night was the space: wooden floors kept the rain warm, concrete made the ferry engine feel close, and a library with carpet swallowed the low synth completely.

      People do not want louder. They want to stop noticing the sound, and then notice it again an hour later.

      What we changed in the app

      • Fade timers now lower each layer at its own pace, which is how the room sounded as the night wound down.
      • Mixes remember headphones and speakers separately.
      • Nights appear in the app 48 hours before tickets go on general sale, for Plus members.

      Next year we want to try mornings: a short set at first light for people who wake before the city does. If you have a room that could host one, write to us.

      Getting started

      Do I need an account?

      No. The free rooms play without one. An account keeps your mixes and timers in step across devices.

      Which devices can I use?

      iOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later, macOS 12 or later, 64-bit Windows 10 or later, most 64-bit Linux desktops, and any current browser through the web player.

      Can I play two rooms at once?

      Not yet. Build a mix instead: the mixer can blend layers from any room you have opened.

      Plus and billing

      How does the free trial work?

      You get fourteen days of Plus. We remind you by email two days before it ends, and nothing is charged if you cancel first.

      How do I cancel?

      Open your account, choose Plan, then Cancel. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

      Can I share Plus with my family?

      Household covers up to five people. Each person keeps their own library, mixes and timers.

      Listening nights

      Are nights free?

      Yes. We ask you to reserve so we know how many chairs to set out. Plus members see new nights 48 hours early.

      Can I host a night?

      If you run a quiet room for forty people or more, write to us with photos of the space and the nights it is free.

      Privacy

      Do you listen through my microphone?

      No. Nightloom never asks for microphone access. It only plays sound.

      What do you collect?

      Your email if you make an account, your saved mixes, and anonymous crash reports you can turn off. The privacy policy has the details.

      About Nightloom

      A small team of sound recordists and engineers making long, slow sound for the hours after ten.

      We started with one room

      In 2021 a recordist with insomnia spent a winter recording the rain outside a rented flat, then wrote a small player that never repeated it. Friends asked for copies. That player became Nightloom.

      We are independent and paid for by Plus. There are no ads, and we do not sell or share what you listen to.

      What we hold to

      • Long over loud

        Rooms run for hours without a loop you can hear. Nothing builds, drops or asks for attention.

      • Leave whenever

        No streaks, no reminders, no feed. Closing the app is always the right thing to do.

      • Recorded, not generated

        Every room starts as a real place, recorded by a person who sat in it all night.
