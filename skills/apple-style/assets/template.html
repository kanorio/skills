    Headphones

    How long does Crest take to charge?

    About two hours from empty with a 20 W USB-C charger. Five minutes of charging gives up to four hours of listening.

    How do I connect Crest to two devices?

    Pair it with the first device, then hold the noise button for five seconds to pair the second. Crest switches to whichever device starts playing.

    Earbuds

    Which ear tip size is right for me?

    Open the Tamber app and run the fit test. It plays a short sound and tells you whether each tip seals well.

    Speakers

    Can I pair two Room speakers?

    Yes. In the Tamber app, choose one Room, then Pair. The two speakers play as left and right.

    Does Room need Wi-Fi?

    No. Room plays over Bluetooth. Wi-Fi adds multi-room playback and software updates.

    Product news

    Meet Room, a speaker that fills the room and not the shelf

    November 4, 2026

    Room is a 22 cm cylinder that plays in every direction. It is available to order today for $279, in Chalk and Graphite.

    Most speakers point at one seat. Room has four drivers facing out and one woofer facing down, so the sound reaches every corner and the bass uses the floor. It measures the room when you place it and tunes itself in about ten seconds.

    Simple on top

    A single ring on the top sets the volume. Press it to play or pause.

    • Bluetooth 5.4 for anything that plays
    • Wi-Fi for multi-room playback and updates
    • Two Rooms play as a stereo pair

    We wanted a speaker you place once and then forget about, except when you are listening.

    Product team, Tamber

    Price and availability

    Tamber Room costs $279 and ships in two to four business days. Orders before 31 December include free engraving on the base. Buy Room.
