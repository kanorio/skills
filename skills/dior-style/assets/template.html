    I. The gallery

    Collection · 12 September 2026

    The Quiet Season

    A film in three chapters for the Autumn–Winter 2026 collection. Two minutes and forty seconds, no dialogue.

    I. The gallery

    We filmed in a long gallery with its shutters open and nothing on the walls. The collection hung on stands in the order it was cut, and the light moved across the floor the way it does in January: low, white and slow.

    The first look is the wrap coat in double-faced cashmere. It has no lining and no hidden seams, so the camera stays on its edge, where the two faces of the cloth are turned in and stitched by hand.

    A garment should be quiet enough that you hear the person wearing it.

    II. The cloth

    Between the looks the film rests on the cloth itself: silk satin cut on the bias, wool crepe pleated by hand, faille that holds its shape without a frame. These are the materials of the season, chosen for how they move rather than how they shine.

    III. The garden

    The last chapter leaves the building. The garden behind the atelier is clipped once a year, in the week the film is made, and the fragrance of the season was composed there at dusk.

    The house

    Maison Veranne

    A couture house of forty people: tailors, seamstresses, a pleater, a perfumer and the clients they know by name.

    Odile Veranne opened a two-room atelier above a bookbinder in 2004, with one stand, two seamstresses and a list of eleven clients. The house still works the way it did then: every piece is cut for a person before it is cut for a rail.

    Ready-to-wear arrived in 2012, made in the same ateliers and finished by the same hands. The first fragrance followed in 2016, composed in the garden behind the salon.

    Orders and delivery

    How long does delivery take?

    Two to four working days, complimentary, in a grey box tied with cotton ribbon. Made-to-order pieces take six to eight weeks.

    Can I have an order gift wrapped?

    Returns and exchanges

    How do I return a piece?

    Within 30 days, unworn and with its tags. A client adviser arranges the collection; refunds go back to the original payment.

    Can made-to-order pieces be returned?

    No. They are cut for you, so we fit them until they are right instead.

    Alterations and care

    Are alterations included?

    Hems, sleeves and waists are adjusted by the atelier without charge for any ready-to-wear piece bought from us.

    How should I care for cashmere?

    Hand wash cold or dry clean, dry flat, and fold rather than hang.

    Appointments and salons

    Do I need an appointment to visit a salon?

    No, the salons are open to everyone. An appointment means a private room and your sizes ready.

    How do I get an invitation to a show?

    Ask a client adviser or request an invitation. Seats are limited and confirmed two weeks before.
