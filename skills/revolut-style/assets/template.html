How to book a lane before work

Guides, 4 min read

Early lanes go first. Here is how members get the 06:30 slot without setting three alarms.

Plus members can book seven days ahead and Unlimited members fourteen, so the window opens at midnight a week before. Turn on reminders and the app nudges you when a lane frees up.

How can we help?

Membership

Can I change my plan?

Yes, any month. Upgrades start straight away and we charge the difference for the days left; downgrades start on the 1st.

How do I pause my pass?

In the app, open Your plan and turn on Pause. Your plan stops renewing until you turn it off, for up to three months in a row.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You pay for the plan you choose and nothing else. The physical card is included.

Booking and visits

How far ahead can I book?

Two days on Day, seven on Plus and fourteen on Unlimited. Late sessions open to Unlimited members a day earlier.

What if I miss a session?

Cancel up to an hour before and the visit goes back on your plan. Missed sessions count as a visit.

Can I bring a friend?

Plus includes one guest pass a month and Unlimited two. Send the pass by link; your friend shows it at the desk.

Billing

When am I charged?

On the 1st of each month for monthly plans, or once a year on the day you joined for yearly plans. Day visits are charged when you book.

How do I cancel?

Open Your plan in the app and choose Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you have student prices?

Students with a valid card get Plus for £29 a month. Ask at any desk to check your card.

Privacy and terms

Plain-language summary of how membership works and what we do with your data. Last updated 1 November 2026.

What we collect

Your name, email, home city and plan; the sessions you book and the venues you visit; payment details, which our payment provider holds for us.

Why we use it

To run your membership, hold your bookings, let venues check you in and send the reminders you switch on. We never sell your data.

Membership terms

Plans renew on the 1st and can be paused or cancelled in the app before then. Venues set their own rules, such as swim caps or climbing inductions.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion at any time through the contact page. We reply within thirty days.
