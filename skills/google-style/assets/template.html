    Ask a question

    Common questions

    Who writes the guide?

    Volunteers who live in town. Each place and answer names the day it was last checked, and nobody is paid to be listed.

    Is it free to list my place?

    Yes. Send the form on the "List your place" page; a volunteer visits before the listing goes live.

    Something here is wrong. How do I fix it?

    Use "Suggest an edit" on any place, or write to us from the contact page. Corrections usually go up within a few days.

    Can I advertise here?

    No. There are no ads and no paid placements. Places appear in alphabetical order or by the date they were added.

    How often is the guide updated?

    Answers are checked at least twice a year, and straight away when we hear that something has changed.

    Bin and recycling days

    Checked by a volunteer on 12 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

    Find your day

    Tuesday · green bin and glass box every week, grey bin every other week

    What goes in which bin

    BinWhat goes inCollected
    Green binPaper, card, tins, cans and plastic bottles, rinsedWeekly
    Glass boxBottles and jars, lids offWeekly
    Food caddyPeelings, leftovers, tea bagsWeekly
    Grey binEverything else that is not hazardousEvery other week

    Holidays and storms

    Over the winter holidays every collection moves one day later. When a storm warning is out, crews skip the cliff roads and come back the next working day, so leave your bins out.

    If your bin was missed

    1. Leave it out until 18:00; late rounds still happen after storms.
    2. Check with a neighbour whether their bin went too.
    3. Report it the next morning, with your street and which bin.

    Bulky items

    Sofas, mattresses and white goods are collected on the last Friday of each month. Book at least a week ahead and put them out the night before, off the pavement.

    The winter ferry, explained

    MWritten by Mari Ostend9 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

    From 1 December the ferry runs its winter timetable until the end of February. There are fewer crossings, the last boat leaves earlier, and for the first time the ferry calls at Gull Point on the way out.

    What changes

    DayFrom the slipwayLast boat back
    Monday to Friday7:10, 9:40, 13:00, 16:3017:15
    Saturday9:40, 13:0014:00
    SundayNo crossings–

    The Gull Point stop

    The outward 9:40 and 13:00 boats stop at the Gull Point jetty for five minutes. It is a short walk from there to the start of the cliff path, so you can walk back to town along the top.

    “We would rather run four boats that leave on time than six that wait for the weather.”

    If the wind is above force six, the first boat of the day is cancelled and the rest run if it drops. The harbour office puts a notice on the slipway gate by 6:30.

    Tickets

    Prices stay the same. Return tickets bought in winter are valid until the end of March, and children under five travel free.

    About the guide

    A town guide, written by the people who live here

    Larkhaven is a harbour town of four thousand people. The guide started as a list pinned up in the library; now it is twelve volunteers, a few hundred pages and a short note every Sunday.

    How we keep it useful

    Checked by a neighbour

    Every place is visited and every answer is checked by someone who lives here, with the date on the page.

    Free to read, free to list

    No ads and no paid placements. The guide is run on small donations and a lot of goodwill.

    Short answers first

    Each page starts with the answer, then the detail. If it takes more than three minutes to read, we split it.

    How a place gets listed

    1. Tell us about it. Send the short form with what the place is and when it is open.
    2. A volunteer visits. They write the listing in their own words and take a note of access and hours.
    3. It goes live. You get an email with the page, and you can suggest changes at any time.

    Privacy and terms

    Last updated 1 November 2026

    PrivacyTerms

    Privacy

    The guide collects only what you send us through its forms: your name, your email and what you write. We use it to reply, to arrange a visit or to send the Sunday note you asked for, and nothing else.

    We keep messages for twelve months and newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by writing to hello@larkhaven.example.

    Your saved places, reminders and the light or dark setting stay in your own browser and are never sent to us.

    Terms

    The guide is written by volunteers and checked as often as we can, but opening hours and timetables change. Check with the place before a long trip.

    Listings are free and are written by the guide, not by the places themselves. We may decline or remove a listing that is closed, misleading or unsafe.

    You may share and quote the guide for non-commercial use with a link back to the page.
