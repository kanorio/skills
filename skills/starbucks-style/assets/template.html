Questions about rewards

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Membership is free and you can leave at any time from your account page.

Do leaves expire?

Leaves last six months from the day you collect them. At Grove and Canopy status they never expire.

Can I collect leaves without the app?

Yes. Give the barista the email address on your account, or show your member code from the website.

Our story

Coffee for the corner, since 2011

We opened one counter inside Union Market with a secondhand roaster and a promise to know every regular by name. Six cafés later, we still try.

What we keep doing

  • We buy from people we have met

    Most of our coffee comes from four growers we visit, under contracts set before each harvest.

  • We roast across town

    Every bean is roasted in Larkhaven on Mondays and reaches the cafés within the week.

  • We stay neighborly

    Each café gives its leftover pastries to a food bank nearby, every night.

A room you can stay in

Big windows, real cups, outlets under every table and no rush. Order ahead when you need to run; sit down when you can.

Customer service

Answers to common questions, and a form for everything else. We reply within one working day.

Orders and pickup

My order was wrong or missing. What now?

Tell the barista and we will remake it on the spot. If you have already left, send us the order number below.

How long does an order ahead take?

Usually five to eight minutes. The confirmation shows a pickup time for your café.

Rewards and account

I forgot my password.

Choose "Account and rewards" in the form and we will send a reset link to the email on your account.

Leaves are missing from my last visit.

Leaves can take up to a day to appear. After that, send us the receipt and we will add them.

Privacy and terms

Last updated January 5, 2026.

Privacy notice

We collect the details you give us (your name, email, birthday month and orders) to run your account, prepare your orders and send the emails you ask for. We do not sell personal information.

Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see your full card number. You can download or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@fernway.example.

Rewards terms

Leaves are collected on qualifying purchases, have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Rewards are subject to availability at each café. We may change the program with 30 days' notice by email.

Gift cards do not expire and can be used in every Fernway café and when ordering ahead.
