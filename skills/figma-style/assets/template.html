Questions

Is there a fee on each booking?

No. You pay the plan price and the card processor's fee on payments. Free classes cost nothing to book.

Can I move from another booking system?

Yes. Export your members and passes as a spreadsheet and we import them, or ask us to do it for you on any paid plan.

Do my members need an app?

No. Your booking page works in any browser, and members sign in with a link sent to their email.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on every paid plan. Starter pages live at your-studio.openhour.example.

What happens to my members if I cancel?

Download everything as a spreadsheet first. Your page stays online until the end of the billing period.

How to fill a Tuesday-morning class

IDInes Duarte
Openhour

Every studio has one: the slot that never fills. We asked the studios with the fullest quiet hours what they changed, and four answers came up again and again.

Name the class for who it is for

"Beginners' wheel, slow pace" filled faster than "Tuesday 10:00". People book a promise, not a time.

The slot did not change. The sentence on the booking page did.

Make the first visit cheaper, not every visit

A single half-price first class brought more regulars than a permanent off-peak discount, and cost far less.

Open the waitlist from the busy class

When Thursday evening is full, offer the people on its waitlist a place on Tuesday morning. Many of them can come.
