The laundry room now books by the half hour

18 November 2026UpdatesSpaces

From Monday, the ten machines on the ground floor of Ferrier Lane book in half-hour slots instead of whole hours. Most washes take forty minutes, so the old hour left machines standing empty.

Open Services, choose Laundry room and pick a time. When your load finishes, the app sends one notification, and if you have not collected it twenty minutes later, the next person can move it to the clean shelf.

What stays the same

  • One credit a load, washer or dryer.
  • The room is open from 7:00 to 22:00.
  • Detergent is still on the shelf by the door.

Help centre

Common questions

10 answers

How do I sign in the first time?Account

Enter your flat number and the six-letter code from your welcome letter. Lost the letter? The front desk can print a new code on weekday mornings.

Can the people I live with use my account?Account

Everyone in the flat can have their own sign-in. On the Household plan they also share your credits; on the others each person books with their own.

I am moving out. What happens to my account?Account

Tell the front desk your moving date. Your bookings after that date are cancelled and your plan stops on the same day.

How far ahead can I book?Booking

The laundry and bikes open seven days ahead, the guest room and the car thirty days ahead, and rooftop beds at the start of each season.

What if I cannot make my slot?Booking

Cancel in the app up to an hour before and the credit comes back. Later than that, the credit is used, so someone else can take the slot.

Something I borrowed is broken.Booking

Put it back on the shelf with the red tag and report it from the booking. Things break; there is no charge for ordinary wear.

When am I charged for membership?Payments

On the day you join and then on the same date each month or year. Your receipts are under Account, Payments.

Can I pay in cash?Payments

Yes, at the front desk from 9:00 to 12:00 on weekdays. You get a printed receipt and the credits appear within the hour.

How do I report a problem in a shared space?Safety

Use Report on the space's page, with a photo if you can. For anything dangerous, call the out-of-hours line printed in every lobby first.

Who can see my name on a booking?Safety

Only the front desk. Other residents see that a slot is taken, never by whom.

About Pebbleway

Made by the people who run the front desk, for the people who live upstairs.

Larch Quarter has a laundry, a roof, a tool shelf and twelve hundred homes. We built one small app so that sharing them takes a minute, not a notice board and a phone call.

How it started

Four dates that explain most of what the app does today.

  1. 2019The residents' association opens the tool shelf in a broom cupboard. Borrowing is a paper list on the door.
  2. 2022The laundry booking sheet fills up by Monday lunchtime. Ines Okafor writes the first version of the app over a winter.
  3. 2024Parcel lockers arrive in the lobby and the app starts opening them.
  4. 2026Most homes in the quarter use the app every week. The front desk still answers every message by hand.

House rules and privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026. Sample text: replace it with your own, checked by someone qualified.

House rules

  • Leave shared spaces as you found them, and on time for the next person.
  • Report anything broken; do not try to fix shared equipment yourself.
  • Bookings are for residents and their guests, not for resale.

What we collect

Your name, email, flat and building, your bookings and your payments. We use them to run the services you book and to contact you about them.

Who sees it

The front desk team. We never sell your details, and other residents never see who made a booking.

Your choices

You can download or delete your account from the app at any time, or ask the front desk to do it for you.
