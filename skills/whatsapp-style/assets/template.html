Fix-it notes/Plumbing

Stop a dripping tap in twenty minutes

By Teodor Vance, 12 November, 4 min read

A tap that drips all night is almost always a worn washer or cartridge. It is a twenty-minute job if the water turns off cleanly.

  1. Turn off the valve under the sink, open the tap to drain it and put the plug in.
  2. Prise off the cap and undo the screw under it.
  3. Lift off the handle and unscrew the body with a spanner, holding the spout steady.
  4. Swap the washer for one the same size, then put it all back in reverse order.

TeodorIf the valve under the sink will not turn, stop there. Send us a photo instead of forcing it.tip

Still dripping after a new washer? The seat inside the tap may be worn. That needs a reseating tool, and that is where we come in.
