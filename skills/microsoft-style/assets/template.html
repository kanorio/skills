Plans and billingWhich plan is right for me?

Solo covers one person. Household covers up to six people, each with their own files. Teams that need an admin page should look at the business plans.

Compare plans
DevicesLeaf 13 or Ridge 15: which should I get?

Leaf 13 is a tablet first, with a pen and a keyboard you can take off. Ridge 15 is a laptop with a bigger screen and a longer battery. If you draw or take handwritten notes, choose Leaf.

Account and sign-inReset your password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password and enter your email. The link we send works for 30 minutes.

Plans and billingChange or cancel a plan

Open Account, then Plans. Changes start at the next renewal; if you cancel within 30 days of a yearly renewal, we refund the full year.

Orders and returnsTrack an order or start a return

Every order has a tracking link in its confirmation email. Returns are free for 60 days: print the label from your order page and drop the box at any carrier point.

Apps and filesMove your files from another suite

In Locker, choose Import and sign in to the service you are leaving. Folders, names and sharing settings come across as they are.

Apps and filesShare a Planner board

Open the board, choose Share and add people by email. Everyone gets reminders for the cards assigned to them.

DevicesSet up Huddle Bar in a meeting room

Mount the bar above or below the screen, connect one USB-C cable and sign in with the room account. Calendar invites for that room show a join button.

Business adminAdd people to your team

On the admin page, choose People, then Add. You pay for the new seat from the day it is added.
