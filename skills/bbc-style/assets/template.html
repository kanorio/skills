      Transport

      Night ferry returns to Calvermouth after nine years

      2 hrs agoCalvermouth and Saltmere

      TA
      Tomás Aldana

      Transport correspondent

      The 11pm ferry from Calvermouth to Saltmere will run again from 2 March, nine years after the last late crossing was cut.

      The boat, a 1960s passenger ferry rebuilt over two winters at the Saltmere yard, will make four late crossings a week, rising to seven from June. Tickets for the first week went on sale on Friday morning and were gone by lunchtime, the operator said.

      The council agreed to cover the running costs for the first two years in its February budget, after a petition from hospital and harbour staff who finish their shifts after the last tram.

      What changes from March

      • Late crossings at 23:00 from Calvermouth and 23:30 from Saltmere, Thursday to Sunday
      • Bikes travel free, with space for twelve on each crossing
      • Fares stay the same as the daytime boat

      "People were walking the long way round the estuary after a late shift. Now they will be home in twenty minutes."

      Dee Harlow, who runs the café on Quay Row

      Not everyone is pleased. Neighbours of the Saltmere slipway have asked for quieter engines after 10pm, and the operator says the refit included new silencers and a slower approach to the jetty.

      Related

      Tram extension: what the new timetable means for your commute

      The first sailing on 2 March will be free for anyone who works nights in the valley, the operator said. Timetables are on the quay and on the council's travel page.

        Flood warnings along the lower Calver as the river keeps rising

        Updated 14:32Sunday 23 November

        Calver Daily

        Summary

        • Flood warnings are in place for the lower Calver from Saltmere bridge to the harbour
        • The river passed 3.1 metres at 14:15 and is expected to peak this evening
        • Sandbags are free at the market hall, Brindle Edge fire station and Ashby Rise community centre
        • The towpath is closed between the bridges and the Riverside tram stop is shut
