Customers/Brightfield Logistics

Brightfield cut the time it takes to get paid from 52 days to 19. How a 300-person freight company moved 14,000 invoices a month onto one ledger.

Brightfield Logistics
Industry
Logistics
Headquarters
Rotterdam, Netherlands
Team
300 people, 9 in finance
Products used
InvoicingPaymentsTreasury

The problem

Brightfield sends an invoice for every shipment it moves between ports and warehouses. By last year that meant 14,000 invoices a month, built in three systems and chased by email from a shared inbox.

Customers paid by wire, by card and by check, often for several invoices at once, and matching those payments took the finance team most of the first week of every month.

19 daysto get paid, down from 52
14,000invoices a month, one system
6 hoursto close the month

What changed

Brightfield now creates each invoice from its shipping system through the API the moment a delivery is confirmed. Customers get a hosted page where they can pay by bank transfer or card, or download a PDF for their own approval flow.

New

Partial payments on invoices

Invoices accept several payments until the amount due reaches zero. Each payment appears in invoice.payments.
