Lantern Lake

A cozy night-fishing adventure. Light the lake one lantern at a time, make friends with the fish, and keep the village glowing until morning.

Released 2027.03.141 to 2 playersAges 7 and up

About the game

Overview

Every night the lake goes a little darker. You are Lumo, a round fish with a lantern, and your job is to keep the lights on: catch drifting sparks, trade them for wicks in the stilt village, and light the old lamps around the shore.

There are no timers and no game over. Some nights you fish for an hour, some nights you just watch the reflections. A friend can join on the same screen as a second fish who carries the matches.

Lantern Lake is out now

Our cozy night-fishing adventure is out on console and PC today. Here is what is in the box, what is free, and what comes next.

Three years ago a sketch of a fish holding a lamp went up on the studio wall. Today it is a whole lake, forty lanterns and a village of very particular neighbours. Thank you to everyone who played the demo and wrote to us.

What you get

  • The full lake: five islands, a cave and the stilt village.
  • Two-player mode on the same screen from the start.
  • A free demo with the first island, and your save carries over.

We wanted a game you could play for ten minutes before bed and still feel you did something kind.

Teo Lindqvist, art lead

What comes next

A free spring update adds a lantern festival and a photo mode. We will share the date in the Comet Letter first, so sign up on the home page if you want to know before anyone else.

About the studio

Studio

We are twenty-four people in an old net loft by the sea, making games and toys that a whole family can enjoy in the same room.

Pocket Comet started in 2016 with a phone puzzle game and a borrowed desk. We still prototype on paper first, play-test every week, and ship when the game feels kind, not when the calendar says so.

What we care about

Values

Play together

Every game works with two people on one screen.

Fair to families

No loot boxes, no timers that nag, clear prices.

Tested by players

Nothing ships before real players have tried it.

Support

Help

Quick answers for players and parents. Type a word to filter the questions.

Playing

Where is my save file?

Saves are stored on your device and in your platform's cloud save when it is turned on. Moving to a new device keeps your progress if cloud saves are on.

Can two people play on one screen?

Yes. Every game except Puzzle Post has a two-player mode on the same screen. Connect a second controller and press any button.

The game runs slowly on my PC.

Open Settings, then Display, and choose the Gentle preset. It halves the effects and keeps the art the same.

Buying

How do refunds work?

Refunds go through the store you bought from. For the Comet Plush, write to us within 30 days and we will send a return label.

Do your games have in-game purchases?

No. You pay once and get the whole game. Paid extras, like the soundtrack, are listed on each game page.

For parents

Is there online chat with strangers?

No. Online races in Paper Planet Racers use preset messages only, and you can turn online play off in the menu.

How do I set a play-time limit?

Use your console's or phone's family settings. Our games save automatically when the limit is reached.

What ages are the games for?

Most are for ages 7 and up. Each game page lists the age it is made for and the reading level of its text.

No questions match. Try another word, or write to us.

Still stuck?

Our support desk answers within two working days. Contact support

Privacy and terms

Policy

We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it.

What we keep

Your name and email when you sign up for a play-test, the Comet Letter or write to us. Games keep save data on your device.

Children

We do not knowingly collect details from children under 13. Family sign-ups are made by a parent.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or write to hello@pocketcomet.example and we will delete your details.

Play-tests and events

What you see in a play-test stays private until the game is announced. Places at in-person events are free and held for 15 minutes after the start.
