    about the studio

    Tumbletree is the picture-book studio of Ines Varga. Every book is written and drawn at one long table under the window on Orchard Row, then printed nearby on thick, uncoated paper.

    one line, six colours

    Each picture starts as one black line drawn with a brush pen. Then it gets flat colour from a box of six: yellow, orange, green, blue, brown and grey. Nothing more, so a two-year-old can find the owl on every page.

    The words come last. A page says one thing, in a sentence short enough to read in one breath, so it sounds right the hundredth time too.

    for grown-ups

    Answers about ordering, visits and the play page. If your question is not here, write to us.

    How quickly do books arrive?

    Books leave the studio within three working days. Delivery time and cost depend on where you are, and we confirm both by email before you pay.

    Can I collect from the studio?

    Yes. Choose "collect at the studio" in your bag and come by Thursday to Saturday, 10:00 to 17:00.

    What happens at a read-aloud morning?

    Ines reads one book twice, slowly, then everyone draws one thing from it. Grown-ups stay. Paper and crayons are on the table.

    Does the play page collect anything?

    No. There are no accounts, ads or purchases on the play page, and the memory game forgets everything when you close it.

    Can a school or library book a visit?

    Yes, for classes of children aged 5 to 7. Tell us your preferred dates and the size of the class.

    Can my shop stock the books?

    Yes. Use the contact form, choose "bookshop" and we will reply with trade terms.

    what we keep

    When you order, book a seat, write to us or sign up for the letter, we keep your name, email and what you sent, and for orders your address. The play page keeps nothing.

    why

    Only to answer you, deliver your books, hold your seats and send the letter you asked for. We never sell or share it for advertising.

    how long

    Orders for as long as tax law asks; messages and bookings for one year; the letter until you unsubscribe.

    your choices

    Ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold at hello@tumbletree.example. Every letter has an unsubscribe link.
