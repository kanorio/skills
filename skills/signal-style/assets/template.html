Why use Evenpath?

Here is what changes when the map knows where the steps are.

Routes Without Guesswork

Every route avoids stairs, steep kerbs and broken lifts, and tells you why it chose each turn.

Volunteers check entrances, ramps and lifts on foot, so the map shows the street as it is today, not as it was drawn years ago.

Lifts You Can Trust

Station lifts report their status every few minutes. If one stops, your route changes before you get there.

Ramps and Kerbs

Gradients, kerb heights and door widths are measured and marked, so you know exactly what is ahead.

Places to Pause

Benches, step-free toilets and quiet corners appear along the way, with opening hours where we know them.

Plan It Together

Send a route to a friend as a simple link. They can open it without an account or an app.

No Ads. No Tracking. No Fine Print.

Routes are planned on your phone. We never store where you go, never sell data and never show ads. Every feature works without an account.

Free Because of People Like You

Evenpath is an independent nonprofit. No investor owns us and nobody can buy us. The work is paid for by grants and by donations from people who use the map.

Lift Status, Now Every Five Minutes

Tomasz Brandt on 14 Nov 2026

Stations that share live data now update the map every five minutes. When a lift stops, every route that uses it changes within one update, and anyone already on the way sees a new route before they reach the platform.

Not every station shares data. For the rest, volunteers and riders report lifts as working or out of order, and the map shows when it was last checked, so you can judge for yourself.

A broken lift should cost you a detour, never a stranded afternoon.

Next, we are adding escalators that run in one direction only, starting with the busiest stations.

How Can We Help?

11 answers

Do I need an account?

No. You can plan, save and share routes without one. Saved routes live on your phone.

Which phones does Evenpath work on?

Android 9 or newer and iPhone with iOS 16 or newer. The web map works in any current browser.

How do you know a lift is working?

Stations that share live data report it every few minutes. Elsewhere, volunteers and riders mark lifts as working or out of order, and the map shows when it was last checked.

Can I set my own limits?

Yes. Choose the steepest slope, the highest kerb and the narrowest gap that work for you, and every route respects them.

What if a route is wrong?

Tap Report on any step of the route. A volunteer nearby checks it, usually within a week.

Do you know where I am?

No. Routes are planned on your phone with map data you download. Your location is never sent to us.

What do you collect?

Only crash reports you choose to send and the map corrections you submit, without your name unless you add it.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Evenpath is a registered nonprofit. Whether a gift is deductible depends on where you live; your receipt shows our registration number.

Can I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, at any time, from the link in your receipt email or by writing to us.

How do I become a mapper?

Pick a street near you on the Volunteer page and join a mapping walk. It takes about two hours and no experience.

Where is the source code?

Every line of the apps and the routing engine is public under an open-source license, and anyone can suggest a change.

Our Mission

Everyone should be able to cross their own city without calling ahead. We build the map that makes that possible, keep it free, and never sell the people who use it.

Why a Nonprofit

A map that people trust with their bodies should answer to them, not to advertisers. Being a nonprofit means nobody can buy Evenpath and turn it into something else.

Open Source, Always

The apps, the routing engine and the map data are public. Cities, researchers and other projects are welcome to build on them.

Terms & Privacy Policy

Short, plain and the same for everyone. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Privacy

Evenpath plans routes on your device. We do not collect, store or share your location, your saved routes or your search history.

If you send a crash report or a map correction, we keep it only as long as we need to fix the problem. Donor names and emails are used for receipts and are never sold or swapped.

Terms

Evenpath is offered as is. Streets change, so check conditions on the ground and use your own judgment.

The apps are open-source software and the map data is shared under an open license; both licenses come with the source code.

Questions? Contact us.
