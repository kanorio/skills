General

What is Moonwake?

Moonwake is a free tide app for phones, tablets and computers. It shows when the water will be high or low at more than 3,000 places, today and for the next twelve months.

Who makes it?

Two part-time maintainers and a few dozen volunteers who write code, check data and translate. You can meet them on the about page.

How do you pay for it?

With donations from the people who use it. There are no ads, no paid tier and no investors, and that will not change.

Can I use it to navigate a boat?

No. Predictions are a guide for walks, fishing and photography. Wind and air pressure can move the water by half a metre, so check official notices before you go to sea.

Using Moonwake

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Every saved place keeps a year of predictions on your device. Offline packs add a whole coastline at once.

My beach is missing. Can you add it?

Usually. Send us its name and the nearest harbour through the contact form, and we will check whether a nearby station covers it.

How does sync work without an account?

Each device shows a sync code. Enter it on your other devices and your places stay in step, encrypted end to end.

Can it remind me before low tide?

Yes. Set a reminder on any place, choose how early, and Moonwake sends one quiet notification. The handbook lists every option.

Privacy

Do you track my location?

No. Your location is used on your device to find the nearest place and is never sent to us.

What do you store about me?

Only the crash reports you choose to send. Read the full privacy policy.

Moonwake handbook

Everything you can do in Moonwake, and how to build it yourself.

Getting started

Android

  1. Install Moonwake from your app store or download the APK from the apps page.
  2. Allow location once, or type the name of a beach.
  3. Tap the star to save it. Tides for the next year download in the background.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Install Moonwake from the App Store.
  2. Search for a place or use your location.
  3. Add the widget to your home screen to see the next tide at a glance.

Desktop

  1. Download the installer for Windows, macOS or Linux from the apps page.
  2. Choose Sync with phone and enter the code your phone shows.
  3. Use Print month to get a whole month of tides on one sheet.

Saving places

Search by beach, harbour or town. Moonwake picks the nearest tide station and shows how far away it is, so you know how much to trust the times.

Tip: press and hold a place to pin it to the top of your list.

Reminders

Each place can have its own reminders. They are worked out on your device, so they arrive even without signal.

ReminderWhen it arrivesDefault
Before low waterA set time before the lowest point60 minutes
Before high waterA set time before the highest pointOff
Morning summaryOnce a day with all of today's tides07:00

Offline packs

An offline pack holds a year of predictions for every place along one coastline, usually 2 to 6 MB. Download packs over Wi-Fi from Settings › Offline packs.

Build from source

You need Git and a recent JDK. These commands build the Android app:

Terminal
git clone https://code.moonwake.example/moonwake/android
cd android
./gradlew assembleRelease

To check a store build, compare its fingerprint with the one on the apps page:

Terminal
./scripts/verify-build.sh moonwake-4.2.0.apk

Data format

Every app reads predictions in the same small JSON format, so you can export them and use them anywhere.

JSON
{
  "place": "Gullhaven",
  "datum": "LAT",
  "events": [
    { "type": "high", "time": "2026-11-23T06:12Z", "height": 3.9 },
    { "type": "low",  "time": "2026-11-23T12:31Z", "height": 0.6 }
  ]
}
FieldTypeMeaning
placestringThe name shown in the app
datumstringThe level heights are measured from
eventsarrayHigh and low waters in time order, heights in metres

Translating

Translations live in plain text files, one per language, and every string has a note that explains where it appears. Tell us which language you would like to help with through the contact form.

Moonwake news

Offline packs, a calmer alarm and 40 new harbours

Nov 12, 2026 · Ines Okafor

Moonwake 4.2 is out for phones, tablets and computers. It is the update readers asked for most: tides that work far from any signal, an alarm that does not make you jump, and a long list of harbours that were missing.

Offline packs

Until now, Moonwake kept a year of predictions only for the places you saved. An offline pack downloads every place along a coastline at once, so a new beach on a walking holiday is already there when you arrive. Most packs are between 2 and 6 MB.

A calmer alarm

The reminder sound is now a soft, rising tone instead of a beep. You can also mark a reminder as only once, for the day you want to cross to an island and back.

40 new harbours

Readers sent us 212 suggestions this year. We added the 40 that have a reliable station nearby, including:

  • Ferry Steps and the north side of Crane Bay
  • Six small harbours along the western islands
  • Two river mouths where the old predictions were often late

Predictions are a guide for walks, fishing and photography, not for navigation. Weather can move the water by half a metre.

Update from your app store, or get Moonwake for another device. The full list of changes is in the release notes.

About Moonwake

Moonwake started in 2019 as a weekend project by a lifeguard who was tired of tide tables printed in tiny type on the back of a ferry timetable. Today it is a small open-source project used on beaches in more than 40 countries.

What we promise

  • No ads, no tracking and no account, now or later.

  • Open code and open data, so anyone can check how a prediction was made.

  • Predictions compared with real measurements every month, with the results published.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

In short: Moonwake does not collect personal data. Your places and settings stay on your devices.

What stays on your device

Saved places, reminders and settings are stored only on the device where you create them. Your location is used on the device to find the nearest place and is never sent to us.

Sync

If you turn on sync, your saved places are encrypted on your device before they leave it. Our server stores the encrypted data and cannot read it. Turning sync off deletes it from the server within 24 hours.

Crash reports

When the app crashes it asks whether to send a report. A report contains the app version, device model and what the app was doing, and no personal data. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. Messages sent through the contact form are kept for one year and then deleted.
