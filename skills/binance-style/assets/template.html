    Frequently asked questions

    1What does Spinrank count?

    Plays reported by community, college and independent radio stations, plus plays from independent streaming services that share their logs with us. Each play is matched to one track and counted once.

    2How often does the chart update?

    The live chart refreshes every hour. The weekly chart closes on Sunday at 23:59 UTC and is published on Monday at 09:00 UTC.

    3Can a track pay to chart?

    No. There is no paid placement anywhere on Spinrank. Plans pay for alerts, history and exports, never for position.

    4How do I get my station counted?

    Send us your station name and how you log plays. Most stations connect in an afternoon. Write to us.

    5What is a new entry?

    A track that reaches the top 200 for the first time. It is marked New until its second week.

    6Is Spinrank free?

    The charts, the news and the weekly email are free. Pro adds hourly alerts, full history and exports. See plans.

    The charts

    1Which plays count?

    Plays from stations and services that report to us, matched to one track each. A play has to last at least 30 seconds to count.

    2Why did a track drop after I saw it climb?

    The hourly chart moves with every report. Late reports can arrive up to 72 hours after a play and are added when they do.

    3How do I report a wrong count?

    Send the track, the station and the date from the contact page. We answer within two working days.

    Your account

    1Is there an app?

    Yes, for phone and tablet. It shows the same charts and sends the alerts you set on the website.

    2How do alerts work?

    Follow a track with the star. Free accounts get a weekly summary; Pro sends an alert when a followed track moves five places or more.

    How the chart is made

    1. 1

      Stations report

      Each station sends its play log every hour, from its playout system or a simple upload.

    2. 2

      We match and check

      Every play is matched to one track. Duplicates and test plays are removed, and big moves are checked by hand.

    3. 3

      The chart publishes

      The live chart updates on the hour. The weekly chart and its raw counts go out every Monday.

    Our story

    1. 2014

      Six college stations share one spreadsheet and publish the first weekly chart.

    2. 2017

      Community stations join. The chart passes 200 stations in nine countries.

    3. 2020

      The hourly chart launches, with the full method published alongside it.

    4. 2023

      Independent streaming services start sharing their logs. The app follows in the spring.

    5. 2026

      1,400 stations in 38 countries report every hour.
