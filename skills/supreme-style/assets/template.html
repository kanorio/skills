          about

          corvo opened in 2012 in a garage under a steep street in lisbon, selling decks, wheels and a few shirts printed in the back. the people who came to buy wheels stayed to skate the street, and some of them still work here.

          we make clothes in small runs: a jacket, a few shirts, a pair of pants, a cap at a time, sewn by workshops north of the city. when a run sells out it is usually gone. a few basics come back each season.

          new things come out most thursdays at 11:00 lisbon time, in both shops and online at the same moment. the front page counts down to the next one.

          we also make skate videos, put up shows by friends in the lisbon shop, and keep a mailing list that tells you a day before anything comes out.

          when do new things come out?

          most thursdays at 11:00 lisbon time, online and in both shops at the same moment. the front page counts down to the next one, and the mailing list hears a day before.

          will something come back?

          usually not. a run is made once. a few basics, the tees and the beanie, come back once a season.

          where do you ship?

          everywhere in the eu and the uk. orders leave lisbon within two working days.

          can i return something?

          within 30 days, unworn and with the tags on. socks and anything bought on sale cannot be returned.

          do you sell to other shops?

          a few, by season. write to us from the contact page.
