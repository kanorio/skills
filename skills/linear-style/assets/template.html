Questions

Does it send replies without asking?

Only in the queues where you turn auto-send on. Everywhere else, drafts wait for a person to review them.

Which inboxes does it work with?

Any address you can forward email from, plus the chat widget and the API.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you pick when you sign up. We never use your messages to train models for other customers.

Can we try it on real tickets first?

Yes. The trial runs on your real inbox in draft-only mode, so nothing goes out until you say so.
