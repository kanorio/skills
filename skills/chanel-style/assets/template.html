        Made to measure

        The atelier

        Eleven people cut, sew and set stones in one workroom above the boutique. A coat takes three fittings and about sixty hours.

        How it works

        1. A first conversation

          An hour in the atelier to talk about how you will wear it, choose cloth and take measurements.

        2. The pattern

          Your pattern is drafted by hand and kept on file, so the next piece starts from it.

        3. Two fittings

          A first fitting in calico, a second in the cloth itself. Small changes are made on the spot.

        4. Collection

          Six to eight weeks later the piece is finished, pressed and ready to collect or be delivered.

        Atelier · 12 November

        A season in the workroom

        Every coat in the collection passes through the same four hands before it leaves the building. We spent the autumn watching one of them come together.

        The cloth arrives in September, a dense wool woven for us in one mill. It rests on the cutting table for two days so that it relaxes before anyone measures it, because a cloth that is cut while it is still tense will move once it is sewn.

        Chalk first, scissors later

        The pattern is laid out by hand and traced in chalk. Nothing is cut until the whole layout is checked a second time by someone who did not draw it. The shears are sharpened every Monday.

        A coat is finished when it hangs straight on the form and nobody wants to touch it again.

        Canvas, padding and lining are cut on the same day, then the coat is basted together for its first fitting. Most of the time goes into what nobody sees: the shoulders, the roll of the lapel, the weight of the hem.

        What it costs, and why

        Sixty hours of work, two fittings, and a lining sewn in by hand. We make fewer coats than we could sell, and we repair every one of them for as long as it is worn.

        Since 2009

        The house

        A small house in Paris that makes clothes, scent and jewellery slowly, in one building, and keeps repairing them.

        A few dates

        1. 2009

          One room, one table

          The atelier opens above a bookbinder in the 3rd arrondissement with two tailors and a single cutting table.

        2. 2014

          The first fragrance

          Cendre is composed with an independent perfumer in Grasse after two years of trials.

        3. 2019

          A bench for gold

          A jeweller joins the workroom; every chain since is made from recycled gold.

        4. 2024

          Lyon

          A second boutique opens in a covered passage, with a fitting room for made to measure.

        What we keep to

        Made in one place

        Cutting, sewing, setting and bottling happen in the same building, so the people who make a piece can answer for it.

        Repaired for life

        Bring back anything we made, at any age. Linings, buttons, clasps and stitching are mended in the atelier.

        Few pieces

        Two collections a year, never more than fifteen pieces each, and nothing made only to be discounted.

        Client care

        Questions

        Delivery and returns

        How long does delivery take?

        Two to four working days within the European Union, five to eight elsewhere. Delivery is complimentary and every parcel is signed for.

        Can I return a piece?

        Yes, within 30 days, unworn and with its labels. We collect it from your door. Made-to-measure pieces and opened fragrance cannot be returned.

        Sizes and fit

        How do your sizes run?

        Our tailoring follows French sizing and is cut close at the shoulder. If you are between two sizes, take the larger and we will adjust the sleeves at no cost.

        How do I find my ring size?

        Measure the inside circumference of a ring that fits, in millimetres. Ask client care for a free sizing ring by post.

        Care and repairs

        Do you repair older pieces?

        Always, whatever their age. Bring them to a boutique or write to client care; most repairs take two to three weeks.

        How should I store a coat?

        On a shaped wooden hanger, in the cotton cover it came in, away from sunlight. Brush it after wearing.
