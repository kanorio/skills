Questions, answered

What is Ferrotype?

A streaming membership for documentaries and filmed workshops about craft, food, design and the natural world. Every title is made with independent filmmakers and makers, and new ones arrive every month.

How much does it cost?

One monthly price, with three plans from $X.XX to $X.XX a month. Every plan includes the whole catalog; they differ in how many screens can watch at once and in picture quality.

Where can I watch?

In any modern browser, and on phones, tablets and TVs through the apps. Sign in with the same account everywhere and your place is saved.

How do I cancel?

From your account page, in two clicks. You keep watching until the end of the month you paid for, and there is no cancellation fee.

What are the workshops like?

Each workshop is a short series of lessons filmed in the maker's own studio, with a materials list, project sheets and a monthly live question session.

Can my household watch too?

Yes. The Household and Studio plans let several people watch at once, each with their own profile, list and progress.

About Ferrotype

We make films about people who make things

A small team of editors, filmmakers and makers, funded by members and nobody else.

Ferrotype began as a Friday screening club in a boat shed: a projector, a bedsheet and a few dozen people who wanted to see how a violin, a loaf or a bridge gets made.

Today it is a membership of series, films, workshops and festival talks, still chosen one title at a time by the same few editors.

Workshops are filmed in the maker's own studio, kitchen or yard, with the mess left in. Every lesson has a materials list and a project sheet.

Talks come from the festivals and weekends we sponsor, recorded in full so members who couldn't travel can still be in the room.
