Questions before you buy

Can I add accelerators later?

Yes. The power supply and cooling are sized for four cards from the start. Order a Ridge 48 upgrade and we fit it, or send the machine back and we test it for 24 hours.

Will it run on a normal office circuit?

Two accelerators run on a standard 15 A circuit. For four, we recommend a dedicated circuit and send a checklist for your electrician.

Do you lease or finance?

Yes, over 12, 24 or 36 months for businesses. Ask sales for a quote with your configuration.

    How a twelve-person studio rendered a feature film on three towers

    PRPriya Raman
    4 November 2026 · 7 min read

    Northlight Studio makes animated features with twelve people and no render farm of its own. For their last film, every final frame went out to a rented farm, and every revision meant another invoice and another overnight wait.

    Key takeaways

    • Three Thorn W8 towers replaced a rented render farm for final frames.
    • Average time per final frame fell from 52 to 19 minutes.
    • The machines paid for themselves in about seven months.

    The problem with renting

    Uploading scenes, queueing and downloading frames added most of a day to each revision, so the team rendered fewer revisions.

    What they set up

    • Three Thorn W8 towers with four accelerators each, one per lead artist.
    • A shared 16 TB project drive over 25 GbE, so every tower reads the same scenes.
    • A small queue that sends frames to whichever tower is idle overnight.

    "We stopped asking whether a revision was worth the render. We just render it."

    Hiro Tanabe, render supervisor at Northlight Studio

    The numbers

    MeasureRented farmThree towers
    Time per final frame52 min19 min
    Revisions per shot37
    Monthly render cost$9,400$0 after purchase

    What they would do differently

    Plan the power first. Two of the towers shared a circuit with the kitchen, and the first week of overnight renders tripped it twice. A dedicated circuit fixed it. If you are planning something similar, talk to our engineers before you order.
