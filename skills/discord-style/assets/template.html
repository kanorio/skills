    About the game

    A comet breaks up over a quiet meadow on the one night you planned a picnic. Up to four friends share one blanket and one plan: catch the glowing pieces before they land on the sandwiches. Saves belong to the group, so anyone can start the next picnic.

    Studio life

    How we playtest with forty strangers a month

    The form, the build, the two hours and the spreadsheet afterwards. Everything we learned from eleven playtests.

    AFAda Fenwick
    Mar 2, 2027 · 7 min read

    When we started Deep Sky Diner we had a rule: nobody on the team is allowed to say a level is fun. Only players can. So once a month, forty people we have never met sit down with a build that is not ready, and we watch.

    Who gets a seat

    Anyone in the club can ask for a seat. We pick by date order, then fill the last ten seats with people who have never played a Glowmoth game, because fans forgive things strangers will not.

    • Forty seats, two hours, one build.
    • Ten seats always go to first-timers.
    • Backers get a guaranteed seat.
    “The best note we ever got was four words long: I forgot to eat.”

    What we watch

    We record three things: where people stop talking, where they laugh, and where they open the menu. The menu is the tell. If someone opens it twice in a minute, the level is asking a question it never answers.

    What changed because of you

    Orders now float at eye level, the grill stays on during pauses and two players can share one pan. If you want to see the next one change, save a seat for playtest 3.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Playtests, games, plans and the club. Still stuck? Ask us

    Playtests

    How do I get a seat?

    Join the club and pick the games you want to test. When a playtest opens we email you a link; seats go in the order people ask, and ten are kept for first-timers.

    Do I need a good computer?

    No. Builds run on most laptops from the last five years. Each invite lists the exact requirements.

    Games

    Where can I buy the games?

    Each game page lists where it is sold. The jam games are free and run in a browser.

    What do I need to run them?

    A computer from the last five years with 4 GB of memory, or any current console. Phones run Lantern Hop only.

    Plans

    What does backing the studio pay for?

    Rent, test devices and the playtest servers. Backers cover about a third of our monthly costs.

    Can I cancel?

    Any time, from the link in every receipt. You keep your perks until the end of the period you paid for.

    Club rules

    What are the rules?

    Be kind to other players and to the team, keep unreleased builds private, and report bugs rather than exploiting them in cups.

    How old do I need to be?

    Thirteen or older. Younger players are welcome at in-person events with a grown-up.

    About

    Four people, one big window

    Glowmoth started in 2023 in a rented room above a bakery in Leeds. We still work there, and we still test every game on each other first.

    Why small games

    We love games you can finish with friends on a weeknight. Small games let us try odd ideas, ship them, and listen to the people who play them, instead of disappearing for five years.

    We are self-funded. Sales, backers and the occasional grant pay four salaries and the rent.

    Finishable

    Every game ends. Most in one evening.

    Together first

    If it isn't better with a friend, we cut it.

    No tricks

    No ads, no loot boxes, no selling your data.

    Privacy

    We collect what you give us: your name, email, the games you pick and, for backers, billing details handled by our payment provider. We use it to send playtest invites, the monthly letter and receipts.

    • We never sell your data or show ads.
    • Playtest builds record anonymous play data, never your voice or camera.
    • Ask us to see or delete your data any time at hello@glowmoth.example.

    Terms

    Playtest builds are unfinished and private: please don't share them, stream them or sell them. Be kind in the club; we may remove anyone who isn't. Backer plans renew until you cancel, and you keep your perks to the end of the period.
