    Why we exist

    The problem

    Small farms take the weather, the pests and the price someone else sets. Many sell below what it cost to grow.

    The fix

    Sell together, sell direct, and agree the farm's share before a single seed goes in. Then say it out loud.

    Your part

    Eat what's in season, accept the odd wonky carrot, and tell a friend. That's honestly most of it.

    Why we print the split on every box

    Ask anyone who sells food what a fair price is and they'll talk about you, the buyer. We wanted to start with the person holding the spade.

    When we started, six farms shared one van. Every week someone asked the same question at the door: how much of this actually goes to the farmer? We didn't have a good answer. So we worked it out, and then we printed it.

    How the number is made

    1. Each farm names its price per crate for the season.
    2. We add the real cost of the van, the packing and the paper.
    3. We add one dollar in ten to keep the co-op running. That's the whole recipe.

    If the price goes up, you'll see which line moved.

    Mara, packing shed lead

    What changed

    People started asking better questions. Why is the van line higher in winter? (Longer routes, worse roads.) Why did the farm share jump in June? (A hailstorm, and we agreed to cover it.) The lid became a conversation, which is exactly what we wanted.

    If you run a small food business and want to do the same, we're happy to share the template. Write to us.

    Good questions

    The ones we hear at the door most weeks. Anything else, just ask.

    Ordering

    When do I need to order by?

    Sunday 8 pm for a Tuesday box, Tuesday 8 pm for Thursday, Thursday 8 pm for Saturday.

    Can I swap things I don't eat?

    Yes. Add a note at checkout and we'll swap up to three things for whatever is plentiful.

    Do you deliver to me?

    We cover about an hour's drive around the packing shed. Put your postcode in at checkout and we'll tell you straight away.

    Delivery

    What if I'm not home?

    We leave the box in the shade, out of sight from the street, and send you a photo.

    Where does the packaging go?

    Leave the crate out on your next delivery day. The paper and string go in the compost.

    The farms

    Is everything organic?

    Some farms are certified and some aren't. Each farm's page says how it grows, in plain words.

    Can my farm join?

    If you're small, family-run and within an hour of the shed, we'd love to talk. Write to us.

    Say hello

    Orders, farms, wholesale, a lost crate. A real person in the packing shed reads everything.

    The small print

    Privacy

    We keep your name, email, address and order history so we can deliver boxes and answer questions. We don't sell it, and we don't use advertising trackers.

    Ask us to see or delete what we hold and we'll do it within a month.

    Terms

    Prices include tax. Box contents change with the weather; we swap like for like. If something arrives damaged, tell us within two days and we'll refund or replace it.
