For parents and carers

Bramble Builders is a club first and a showcase second. Here is how we keep it safe, kind and worth the trip.

How we keep it safe

Nicknames only

Members never show full names, photos or schools. A mentor checks every project page before it goes live.

Adults in every room

Two background-checked mentors per group, online and in the studio. Sessions are never one to one.

You see what they see

Every booking, reminder and message goes to a parent or guardian's email address.

Questions parents ask

What age is the club for?

Ages 9 to 16, in three groups: 9 to 11, 12 to 13 and 14 to 16. Each group has its own sessions and mentors.

Does my child need any experience?

No. Most members start with no coding at all. Early sessions use visual blocks, and text code comes when they ask for it.

What do they need to bring?

A laptop if they have one. We lend laptops in the studio, and online sessions run in a web browser.

How much does it cost?

The taster session is free. Membership is paid by the term, and we keep funded places for families who need them. Ask us and we will explain.

Can I see my child's projects?

Yes. We email you a link to every project page, and you can ask us to take a page down at any time.

What can visitors do on the showcase?

Play, favorite and vote. There are no comments and no messages between visitors and members.

Autumn Jam: fourteen games in one weekend

Club newsNov 3, 2026

Forty-two members arrived on Saturday morning with one rule: every game had to fit on a single screen. By Sunday afternoon there were fourteen finished games, three of which now sit in the showcase.

Moonbase Picnic won the vote for the game everyone wanted to play again. Four players share one picnic blanket in low gravity, and the only goal is to keep the sandwiches on the moon. It sounds simple; it is not.

What we learned

  • Small teams finish. Every team of three shipped; one of the two teams of five did not.
  • Playtest early. The teams that swapped games on Saturday evening fixed their hardest bugs before bed.
  • Snacks matter more than we planned for.

The Winter Jam is on Saturday 13 December. Places go to members first, then to anyone who has booked a taster.

About the club

We help young people make the games they want to play, with mentors beside them and friends around them.

Bramble Builders started in 2019 as a Saturday club in a borrowed classroom in Wexcombe. Today about 120 members meet online on weeknights and at our studio in Mill Yard on Saturdays. We are a not-for-profit: membership fees pay for mentors, laptops and funded places.

How a session runs

  1. Show and tellTen minutes to share what changed since last time, however small.
  2. BuildAn hour of making in pairs or small teams, with mentors moving between tables.
  3. PlaytestEveryone plays someone else's game and leaves one kind note and one idea.

Privacy and club rules

Last updated Nov 1, 2026

Privacy notice

We collect a member's nickname and age group, and a parent or guardian's email address. We use them to book sessions, send reminders and show a member's projects under their nickname. We never sell or share them, and we never ask for a member's full name, school or photo.

We keep booking details for as long as a member is in the club and delete them twelve months after the last session. A parent or guardian can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to safety@bramblebuilders.example.

Club rules

  • Be kind in every session, online and in the studio.
  • Use your nickname, never your full name.
  • Ask before you use someone else's work, and credit them.
  • Mentors have the last word on what goes in the showcase.
