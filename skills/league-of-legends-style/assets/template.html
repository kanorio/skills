      Rules 1.3 is live: lanterns burn longer

      18 Nov 2026/Odette Marsh, lead designer

      Rules 1.3 is the first update since the campaign book went to print. It is small on purpose: three changes, two clarifications and one new set of cards for five-player tables.

      What changed

      Maren's lantern now reaches three spaces. At two, tables kept leaving her behind, and a warden who cannot keep up is not much of a warden. Ilka's Shatter costs two focus, because one focus made her the answer to every question.

      The full list, with the old and new wording side by side, is on the rules updates page. Printed cards come with the next reprint, and the files are free to print now.

        Rules

        Can we play with fewer than five heroes?

        Yes. Every chapter scales from one to five players. With one or two, each player controls two heroes.

        What happens when a hero falls?

        They leave the board until the next dawn phase and take a scar card. Scars carry through the campaign, so a fallen hero changes the story.

        Orders and shipping

        The studio behindThe long night

        Brasshollow Games is four people in a converted print shop in Bristol. Gloamreach is our first game, and it took five winters of playtests to get here.

        How it started

        1. 2022

          A kitchen-table prototype with coins for heroes and a hand-drawn map.

        2. 2024

          Two hundred playtests at cafés, libraries and one very patient wedding.

        3. 2025

          The art, the campaign book and the first print run.

        4. 2026

          The Drowned Coast, and campaign nights in four cities.

        • Win together

          Nobody plays against anybody. The night is the only opponent at the table.

        • Made to play twice

          Scars, allies and omens change each campaign, so the second one tells a different story.
