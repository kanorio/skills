    Why we still run a late show on Fridays

    Mara Quell · 14 November 2026 · 6 min read

    Every Friday at 22:40 the big room fills with people who have already had dinner, already had a drink and are ready to be frightened a little. The late show is our oldest habit, and the one we get asked about most.

    It started as a way to use the room after the last regular screening. It stayed because the audience is different at that hour: louder, kinder and more willing to laugh at the parts that were meant to be serious.

    A late show is not a film you watch alone. It is a film you watch with two hundred strangers who are all about to jump at the same moment.

    We pick the films months ahead, mostly cult favourites and restorations, with one new film each season. If there is a title you want to see on the big screen near midnight, tell us at the bar.

    Midnight Matinees runs every Friday and Saturday. Members on the Night Owl plan get in free.

    Getting here

    Tram 4 stops across the road. Bike racks are by the side door; the harbour car park is five minutes away.

    The building

    Opened in 1936 as one 600-seat room; now two screens, a bar in the old foyer and a balcony that still creaks.

    The bar

    Coffee and cake in the day, cocktails and local beer at night, and popcorn popped in the foyer every hour. Take your drink into any screening.

    Help and FAQ

    Tickets, membership and getting around the building. Can't find it? Ask at the bar or write to the box office.

    Tickets and booking

    How do I book?

    Pick a film in What's on, choose a time and how many tickets you need. Your tickets arrive by email; show them on your phone at the door.

    Can I change or refund a ticket?

    Swap to another showing up to two hours before the film starts, at the box office or by replying to your booking email. Cancelled shows are refunded in full.

    Do you show films with subtitles?

    Every Wednesday screening has English subtitles, and films in other languages are subtitled at every show.

    Membership

    Is membership worth it if I come once a month?

    The Regular plan includes one free film a month and $7 tickets after that, so it usually pays for itself by the second film.

    Can I pause my membership?

    Pause for up to three months a year from your account. Your perks wait for you.

    Can I bring a guest?

    Projectionist members can bring a guest at member prices, including to members' previews.

    The building

    Is the building step-free?

    Yes. Both screens, the bar and the toilets are step-free from the street, and a companion or carer comes in free.
