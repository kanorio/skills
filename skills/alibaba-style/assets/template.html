                Help center

                Ordering, samples, payment and freight, answered.

                Ordering and minimums

                What is a minimum order?

                The smallest quantity a workshop will make in one run. It is on every product, next to the price ladder.

                Samples

                How do samples work?

                Order one of any item from its product page. It ships in five working days, and the price comes off your first order of that item.

                Payment

                How do I pay?

                A 30% deposit by bank transfer or card starts production; the balance is due when we send photos of the finished goods.

                Do you offer payment terms?

                After three completed orders, trade accounts can apply for 30-day terms.

                Shipping and freight

                Which delivery terms do you quote?

                FOB from the workshop's port, or delivered duty paid (DDP) to your door. Sea freight is free on orders over $3,000 and takes three to six weeks.

                Quality and returns

                What if goods arrive damaged?

                Send photos within 14 days of delivery. We replace or refund damaged units in the next shipment.

                What we do

                We are a small trading company that sits between independent workshops and the cafés, hotels and shops that buy from them. We find the makers, agree fair prices by quantity, check every run and handle the paperwork and freight, so a buyer deals with one company and one invoice.

                Every product in the catalog comes from one of six partner workshops we have visited in person. When you need something we do not list, we ask them first.

                How we check quality

                We audit each workshop once a year, approve a golden sample before every new run, and inspect a share of every order before it leaves: finish, size, colour, packing and carton labels. You get the inspection photos with your balance invoice.

                  Privacy

                  We use the details you send us to answer inquiries, prepare quotes and deliver orders. We share them with the workshop making your order and the freight company carrying it, and with no one else.

                  Ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold about you, or to delete it.

                  Terms of trade

                  Prices on this site are estimates by quantity tier and exclude freight and import duties. A quote is valid for 30 days. Production starts when we receive the deposit; the balance is due before shipping.
