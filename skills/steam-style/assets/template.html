          About this game

          Every night the lamp needs oil, every morning the nets need mending, and every week a new ship asks for something you do not have yet.

          Trade, build and restore the old harbor at your own pace. There is no timer and no way to lose, only the next storm to get ready for.

              Ember Lighthouse 1.2 brings winter storms and a second keeper

              18 Nov 2026 · Quillpine

              Winter has reached the rock. Storms now build over three nights instead of arriving all at once, so you can see them coming in the clouds and get the shutters up in time.

              A second keeper can now share the lighthouse in online co-op. One of you trims the lamp while the other trades on the pier; your progress saves to the host's world.

              Smaller fixes

              • The lamp remembers your oil setting between nights.
              • Ships no longer queue behind the pier at high tide.
              • Controller prompts match the controller you are holding.
