The ten-minute habit

Short, daily practice beats a long weekend session. Here is how to make it stick without a fight.

ICInes CallowayFounder and former primary teacher · 6 min read

Children forget fast and remember fast. A word met on Monday and again on Tuesday is far more likely to stay than the same word met five times on a Saturday. That is the whole idea behind a ten-minute lesson.

pick a time, not a goal

"Every day" is a wish. "After brushing teeth" is a plan. Tie the lesson to something that already happens, and it will happen too.

  • Right after breakfast, before shoes go on.
  • In the car on the way to practice.
  • As the last thing before the bedtime story.

A missed day is fine. A streak freeze covers it, and nobody loses their fox.

Help

questions, answered.

The things parents ask us most. Something missing? Write to us.

lessons

How long is a lesson?

About three minutes, made of five to eight short questions. Most kids do two or three a day.

Does my child need to read?

Not for the 5 to 7 courses. Every word is spoken aloud and most answers are pictures.

Who writes the lessons?

Primary teachers who speak the language at home, then native speakers record the audio.

accounts and safety

Are there ads or chats?

No ads, and no way to message other users. Leaderboards show first names only, and you can turn them off.

What do you store about my child?

A first name, an age group and lesson progress. Nothing else. Read the privacy policy.

plans and billing

Can we stay on the free plan?

Yes, for as long as you like. The free plan has one language and one lesson a day.

How do I cancel?

From the grown-up settings, in two taps. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

About us

made by teachers, drawn with care.

We started in a classroom with a puppet fox and a box of picture cards. The cards became an app; the fox stayed.

small steps, every day.

Kids learn a language the way they learn a song: a little at a time, again and again, with someone cheering. Mossberry is built around that, from the length of a lesson to the sound the chest makes.

what we promise.

kind, always

No timers that shout, no shame for mistakes. Wrong answers come back later, gently.

real teaching

Every lesson is planned by a teacher and checked by a native speaker.

no tricks

No ads, no in-app purchases for kids, and no selling of anyone's data.
