Support

Answers about ordering, delivery, charging and home energy.

How does ordering work?

Design your car, pay the $250 order fee and an advisor calls within one working day. You pay the balance or sign the finance agreement before delivery.

Can I change my car after ordering?

Yes, until it is scheduled for production. Change the paint, wheels or interior from your account; the price updates and the order fee carries over.

When will my car arrive?

New orders usually arrive in four to six weeks, cars from inventory within two. Your delivery date appears in your account once the car is built.

How long does charging take?

About 15 minutes at a fast charger to add 300 km. At home, a wall charger fills the battery overnight.

Do I need a charger at home?

Most owners install one. A wall charger adds about 60 km of range per hour; a standard socket works for short daily trips.

How do I book a service?

Book from the app or call your nearest service centre. Many jobs are done at your home by a mobile technician.

What does the warranty cover?

Four years or 80,000 km on the car, and eight years or 160,000 km on the battery and motors, whichever comes first.

Does Hearth work during a power cut?

Yes. It detects the outage and takes over in a fraction of a second, so the lights stay on and the fridge keeps running.

How long does a solar installation take?

Installation is one or two days. Permits and the grid connection usually take four to eight weeks before that.
