How a video gets made

  1. Make it twice

    Once to learn it, once to find the four steps that matter.

  2. Film from above

    One phone on a clamp, one lamp through baking paper.

  3. Cut to thirty seconds

    Every step gets a caption you can read with the sound off.

  4. Answer the comments

    The best questions become the next video.

Questions

Can I use your videos in my class?

Yes, show them in a classroom or a workshop as often as you like. Please do not re-upload them; link to them instead.

Do you sell the things you make?

Sometimes, at the Saturday market. We do not run a shop; the point is that you can make them yourself.

Do you take requests?

All the time. Leave a comment under any video; about one in five of our videos started as a question.
