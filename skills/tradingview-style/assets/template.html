      Eggs are up 18% in a year. Two shops kept them under $4

      IVInes Varela
      Price desk · Thursday 19 November 2026 · 4 min read

      A dozen eggs cost $4.86 at the middle of Wrenhaven's shelves this week, up from $4.10 a year ago. That is the biggest yearly rise of anything in our basket, and it has not slowed: this was the third week in a row of increases.

      EgEggsEGG124.86USD+3.20%
      +18.4%in a year
      14stores checked
      2still under $4

      Where they are still under $4

      Two shops in Saltmarsh kept a dozen below four dollars all autumn. Both buy from the same farm on the edge of town and sell the eggs loose, so there is no carton price to pass on.

      “We put the price on a chalkboard every Monday. It has gone up twice since summer, ten cents each time.”Shop owner, Saltmarsh

      What happens next

      Wholesale prices usually ease after the winter. We will keep checking every week; if you see eggs for less, report the price and we will add it once we can match it to a shop.

      How we measure

      Volunteers visit the same shops, cafés and petrol stations every week and note the shelf price of the same item in the same size. Rents come from new listings, bills from published tariffs. Readers' receipts count once we can match them to a shop, marked R.

      The basket is forty items weighted by what a typical household spends, set to 100 in January 2024. Red means something costs more than before, green that it costs less. Mistakes are fixed in place and listed in the next Friday email.

      Questions

      Does any shop pay you?

      No. Readers and partners fund us. We do not take advertising or sponsorship from anyone whose prices we track.

      Can I use your numbers?

      Yes. Quote them with a link to the page. Schools, libraries and newsrooms can get every series as a spreadsheet as partners.

      Why is my receipt different?

      We publish the middle shelf price, so half the places we check are cheaper. Loyalty-card offers are left out.

      How do I report a mistake?

      Write to us from the contact page and we will fix it within a working day.

      Privacy notice

      Last updated 1 November 2026.

      Pricemere keeps as little about you as it can. This notice says what we collect, why, and how to have it removed.

      What we collect

      Your email address if you sign up for the Friday email or join as a supporter; the price, shop, date and neighbourhood when you report a price; your name and message when you write to us. We do not use advertising trackers.

      Why

      To send the email you asked for, to check reported prices, and to answer you. Reports are published only as part of a middle price, never with your name.

      Your choices

      Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To see or delete what we hold, write to desk@pricemere.example and we will reply within a month.
