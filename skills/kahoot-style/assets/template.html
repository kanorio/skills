      Question writing

      How to write a question that is hard but fair

      LRLucía Romero19 Feb 2026

      A good question has one answer, and most of the room has met it before. The difficulty should come from remembering, not from guessing what the writer meant.

      We test every question on two people who did not write it. If either of them can argue for a second answer, the question goes back to the desk.

      Finally, read it aloud. A question that looks clear on paper can be a tongue-twister on the microphone.

      Questions, answered

      The short version of everything people ask before their first night.

      Quiz nights

      How big can a team be?

      Two to eight players. Bigger groups split into two tables and play against each other, which is usually more fun anyway.

      Do I need to book?

      Booking holds a table until the first question. Walk-ins are welcome when there is room.

      Study club

      Is the first session really free?

      Yes. Come once, and pay for the term only if you want to stay.

      Team quizzes

      How far ahead should we book?

      Three weeks for a Huddle, six for an Offsite or a Festival, so there is time to write your custom rounds.

      Can you run it online?

      Yes. Players answer on any device and the host runs the show on a video call you choose.

        We train hosts to read a room, not just read a screen.
