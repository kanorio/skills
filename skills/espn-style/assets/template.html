                Game recap · Round 9

                Calder scores 24 as the Gulls beat the Comets and clinch a playoff place

                Port Alder closed on a 9-2 run for a 78-71 win, its fourth in a row, and is guaranteed a top-two finish in the North.

                CB

                Callie BrandtSenior writer · Nov 21, 2026, 9:48 PM

                Ada Calder scored 24 points and grabbed nine rebounds, and the Port Alder Gulls closed on a 9-2 run to beat the Cinder Hill Comets 78-71 on Saturday night at Harbor Hall.

                The win is the Gulls' fourth in a row and guarantees them a top-two finish in the North Division, which means a place in the semifinals in March.

                Cinder Hill led 53-52 after a three from Juno Brisco late in the third quarter. Port Alder answered with eight straight points, and with the game tied at 69, Calder's jumper with 1:40 left started the closing run.

                "We didn't change anything at the end. We kept getting stops and trusted the next pass."

                Ada Calder, Gulls guard

                Brisco finished with 19 points and eight assists for the Comets, who fall to 6-4 and two games behind in the North.

                  About the league

                  The Tideline League is women's basketball for eight towns on the coast. Teams play fourteen league games from October to February, a knockout cup before the holidays, and the top four meet in March.

                  • 8teams
                  • 14league games each
                  • 2011first season
                  • 4playoff places

                  How a season works

                  1. September. Open tryouts and preseason camps in every town.

                  2. October to February. Fourteen league games, home and away against every team.

                  3. November and December. The Tideline Cup, a knockout for all eight teams.

                  4. March. The top two in each division play the semifinals, then the final.

                  Questions

                  Where can I watch games?

                  Every game is in a home gym, and highlights are posted on the news page the same night.

                  Do children need tickets?

                  Under-12s pay half price with an adult, and under-5s sit on a lap for free.

                  How do we enter a team?

                  Register by March 31 on the join page. You need a coach, a home court and at least ten players.

                  What happens when a game is postponed?

                  We post the new date on the scores page within a day, and tickets move to it automatically.
