Ines Calloway nearly cut the bridge from Paper Boats. She plays us the demo without it, the four rewrites, and the night it finally made sense on stage.

Chapters

Show notes

Paper Boats is the second song on Weather for Two, out April 2. Ines wrote the verses in a single evening and spent the next month on eight bars in the middle.

  • Songs in this episode: Paper Boats, Low Pressure
  • Recorded in Room 4, February 2027
  • Produced by Wren Albescu, edited by Jonah Fell
Read the opening of the transcript

Wren: You sent me a voice memo at two in the morning that just said "no bridge". What happened?

Ines: I'd played it to a friend and she looked at her phone right at that point. So I thought, it goes. It took me a month to realise she was looking up the lyrics.

About

A small studio by the river

Driftwire is nine people, two rooms and a lot of microphones in Bristol. We make podcasts we would listen to and put out records by artists we have worked with for years.

How we work

Every show starts as a conversation in Room 4. We record more than we need, edit slowly and publish on the same day every week, so you always know when the next one lands.

The records come from the same room. Artists who guest on our shows often come back to make something longer, and we release it on our own label with the people who made it paid first.
