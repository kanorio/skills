01Heritage

It started with a pencil line

Fifty-five years, one town, and a rule we have never broken: a car leaves Calvera only when the person who built it would drive it home.

  1. 1971

    A two-car garage

    Two engineers rent a garage on the edge of Calvera and build a single coupé for a hill climb. It finishes second. They keep going.

  2. 1984

    The first endurance win

    Six hours, two drivers, one set of brakes. The team learns that finishing is a way of winning.

  3. 1998

    The atelier opens

    Owners ask for their own colours and leathers. A room next to the line becomes the atelier, and no two cars leave alike again.

  4. 2016

    Carbon, by hand

    The first carbon tub is laid up in Calvera. It takes nine days. It still does.

  5. 2026

    Vertice

    The lightest car we have made, and the first to carry the race team's gearbox on the road.

Racing · 14 October 2026 · 4 min read

Four hours in the rain at Lindmark

The rain arrived with forty minutes gone and did not leave. By the end, both Vertices were home and one of them was first.

Lindmark is a fast circuit in the dry and a strange one in the wet: the water gathers on the inside of the long right-hander and the racing line moves a car's width outwards. The crews knew it. The question was when to change tyres.

Nine minutes of light

With the sun going down behind the main grandstand, the pit wall had a choice: stop now on intermediates, or wait for the track to dry and risk the last stint in the dark. They waited one more lap than anyone else.

We did not need to be faster. We needed to be on the right tyre for twenty minutes longer than everybody else.

Car #7 took the lead at the final stop and held it to the flag. Car #8 lost a lap behind a safety car and recovered to fourth. Full results are on the racing page.
