              Common questions

              How long does a repair take?

              Most screen, battery and keyboard repairs are ready within five working days. If a part has to be ordered we tell you the date before we start.

              Do you repair other brands?

              Yes. We repair most laptops, desktops, monitors and printers, whoever made them. You get a fixed quote after diagnosis and nothing is charged until you agree.

              My printer will not connect to Wi-Fi

              Restart the router and the printer, then run the wireless setup from the printer's screen. Our guide walks through each step, and the store can help over the phone.

              Will you move my files to a new computer?

              Yes, for free with any computer bought here. Bring the old machine and we copy documents, photos and browser bookmarks across.
