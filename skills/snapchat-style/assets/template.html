Learn to roll in four Sundays

Ninety minutes a week, groups of eight, two coaches. Skates and pads included.

  1. 1Stand, fall, get upReady stance, falling forward on your pads and getting up without help.
  2. 2Glide and stopPushing from the heel, gliding on two feet and a toe-stop drag.
  3. 3TurnsLeaning into the curve and your first crossover at walking pace.
  4. 4Your first full sessionJoin the open floor with a coach beside you for the first ten laps.
  1. 1Better stopsPlough stop and T-stop, then stopping on a line.
  2. 2BackwardsSwizzles backwards and the turn from front to back.
  3. 3CrossoversBoth directions, then at speed through the corners.
  4. 4Rink sensePassing, spacing and the rules of a busy Friday floor.
  1. 1SpinsTwo-foot spins, then a spin out of a glide.
  2. 2FootworkShuffles, the grapevine and heel-toe steps.
  3. 3Low and fastA first squat glide, held for a count of eight.
  4. 4Put it to musicA thirty-second routine to one of Juno's records.

A rink under the old rail bridge

Four friends, one empty arch and a lot of second-hand maple.

Moonroll opened in an empty railway arch with a floor we laid ourselves and forty pairs of skates from a rink that was closing down. We wanted somewhere to skate on a Friday that felt like a party, and somewhere to learn on a Sunday that didn't feel like a test.

Today there are sixty rental pairs, a deck on the river and a crew of four who still lace up every week.

Ask us anything

Questions

The things people ask at the counter.

Do I need my own skates?

No. Rentals from size 26 to 46 are included in the price of every session except Late Laps.

What should I wear?

Clothes you can bend your knees in and tall socks. Pads are free to borrow, and we recommend them for your first few nights.

I've never skated. Is that all right?

Most Saturdays are full of first-timers. If you want to learn properly, the four-Sunday course is the quickest way.

Can children skate?

Yes, on family afternoons and open skates. Under 12s skate with a grown-up on the floor; helmets are free and required for under 16s. Late Laps and Glow Night are 18 and over.

Can I bring a group?

Groups of ten or more get the party room and a crew member. Tell us the date.

Can I pay at the door?

Yes. Saving a spot online keeps skates in your size, and you pay when you arrive.

Book the rink, or just say hi

Birthday parties, school trips, private hire and everything else. We answer within two days.

Privacy

We collect only what you type into our forms: your name, email or phone number, and the details of your booking. We use them to hold your spot, answer you and send the Friday line-up if you asked for it.

What we keep

Guest list sign-ups are kept until you ask us to remove them. Booking messages are deleted a year after the event. We don't sell or share them.

Your choices

Write to hello@moonroll.example to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you.

Terms

Skating is a sport with falls. By joining a session you agree to follow the crew's directions and the flow of the floor.

Sessions and spots

A saved spot holds skates in your size until thirty minutes after the session starts. Payment is taken at the door.
