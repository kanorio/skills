Getting here

Shuttle

Loops from six valley towns every twenty minutes, 1 pm to 2 am. One pass covers all three days.

Drive and park

Parking opens at noon. Cars with four or more people park free in the carpool lot.

Ride share

Drop-off and pick-up at the North Gate lot, a ten-minute walk to the entrance.

Bike valet

Free, guarded bike parking at the East Gate until an hour after the last set.

Camping

Wake up ten minutes from the stage.

The campground opens Thursday at 3 pm and closes Monday at noon. Every site is flat, numbered and close to water.

  • Hot showers and lockers in each camping field
  • A general store for ice, coffee and forgotten tent pegs
  • Quiet camping for people who want to sleep before 2 am

What to pack for three days in the desert

Days reach 34°C and nights drop to 12°C. Pack for both, and pack light: you carry everything from the shuttle or the car.

RLRosa Lindgren, site operations · April 18, 2027 · 6 min read

Every year we watch thousands of people walk through the North Gate, and every year the happiest ones carry the least. Here is what the crew packs after seven summers on the flats.

The short list

  • A refillable bottle, empty when you come through the gate
  • A wide hat and sunscreen you will actually reapply
  • A warm layer for after dark
  • Shoes you can stand in for ten hours
  • Earplugs and a small power bank

What to leave at home

Glass, umbrellas, drones, folding chairs with arms and anything with a speaker bigger than your hand. The full list is in the questions on the grounds.

Bring a layer. The people shivering through the last set are always the ones who were sure they would not need it.
Rosa Lindgren

Help

Questions, answered

Passes Getting here On the grounds Accessibility Health and safety

Passes

Can I give my pass to someone else?

Yes, until June 1. Transfer it from your account and the new holder gets a fresh wristband in the post.

How do the four payments work?

You pay a quarter today and the rest on the same date over the next three months. The pass is yours from the first payment.

What if I lose my wristband?

Come to the box office at the North Gate with photo ID. We deactivate the old one and print a new one on the spot.

Getting here

Where does the shuttle stop?

In six towns along the valley road. Stops and times are on the plan your trip page.

Can I leave my car overnight?

Only in the camping lots. Day parking closes an hour after the last set, so plan a ride if you might stay late.

On the grounds

What can I bring in?

A small bag, an empty refillable bottle, sunscreen, a hat and a blanket. Leave glass, umbrellas, drones and large speakers at home.

Is water free?

Yes. Twelve refill stations are open from gates to close, and every bar pours water for free.

Can I bring a chair?

Low folding chairs without arms are fine on the lawns. They stay folded in front of the stages.

Accessibility

Are the grounds step-free?

The main walk, every stage and the accessible camping field use firm matting. Golf carts run from both gates all day.

Do you have viewing platforms?

Yes, at all four stages, with space for one companion each. Register your access needs before May 15 so we can plan the space.

Can I bring a support person?

Yes. Support people come in free with a registered access pass. Write to the access team from the contact page.

Health and safety

Where is first aid?

Next to the Lantern Garden, with a second post in the camping fields. Both are open from gates until two hours after close.

What happens if it gets too hot?

We open extra shade tents, add misting stations and move early sets back. Updates go out on the stage screens and in the newsletter.

Still looking for an answer?

About

A gathering, not a show.

It started in 2019 with forty friends, two speakers and a borrowed generator at the edge of Juniper Flats. The idea has not changed: music sounds better outside, with people you have not met yet.

Small on purpose.

We cap each day at 18,000 people, so lines stay short and you can find your friends again after the last set. Every stage is a short walk from the next, and every one of them faces the mountains.

What we keep to

Three promises to the valley.

Leave it better

Solar power runs two stages, and the crew stays a week after close to walk every field.

Every ear welcome

Step-free paths, viewing platforms and free entry for support people, planned with the people who use them.

Local first

Two of every three food stalls come from the valley, and so does most of the crew.
